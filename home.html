01 / Research

Three connected
research directions.

From geometric representation to numerical approximation, our work studies how mathematical structure shapes computation.

Faculty research overview↗
01

Geometry & singularities

Geometric representation meets numerical analysis.

Isogeometric, generalized finite element, and mapping methods for differential equations in domains with cracks, corners, and boundary layers.

Isogeometric analysisGeneralized FEMMapping methods
02

Fractional differential equations

Numerical algorithms for fractional-order models.

Efficient methods for fractional initial and boundary value problems, nonlinear time-fractional equations, and fractional dynamical systems.

Fractional dynamicsTime-fractional PDEsFast algorithms
03

Computational mechanics

Approximation for models of solid deformation.

Numerical approximation for elasticity and plate models, including problems with cracks, singularities, and fourth-order partial differential equations.

ElasticityPlate modelsSingular PDEs
