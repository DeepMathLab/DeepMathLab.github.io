01 / Research

Research directions

Three connected directions, grounded in applied mathematics and scientific computing.

01 Computational solid mechanicsMathematical models of deformation and singularities +

Numerical approaches to solid mechanics problems, including plate models, cracks, and singularities. We investigate how mathematical structure informs computational methods.

Solid mechanicsSingularitiesGeneralized FEM
02 Isogeometric analysisConnecting geometry and numerical approximation +

Isogeometric analysis connects geometric representations with numerical approximation. Related interests include generalized finite element, collocation, and meshless methods.

Isogeometric analysisCollocationMeshless methods
03 Fractional differential equationsEfficient methods for fractional-order models +

Efficient numerical algorithms for fractional differential equations and dynamical systems, including time-fractional partial differential equations and nonlinear problems.

Fractional equationsDynamical systemsNumerical algorithms
