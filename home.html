02 / People

A mathematical perspective
on scientific computation.

DeepMathLab is a computational mathematics research group at KENTECH, led by Hyunju Kim. We study the relationship between the structure of differential equations and the numerical methods used to solve them.

Our interests connect geometric approximation, fractional-order equations, and computational mechanics. This website brings together the group’s research themes, people, and selected published work.
